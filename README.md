Rapid urbanization and global climatic warming have significantly intensified extreme urban heat 
hazards across South Asian metropolitan areas. The National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi 
regularly experiences dangerous pre-monsoon heat extremes exceeding 42 °C to 48 °C, 
presenting severe physiological stress to outdoor manual laborers, elderly residents, and critical 
municipal energy infrastructure. Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) simulations require hours 
on supercomputers and lack localized feature responsiveness. This project designs and 
implements an end-to-end, high-performance distributed machine learning pipeline using Apache 
Spark (PySpark 4.3.0) and Spark MLlib to forecast next-day maximum temperatures and detect 
extreme heat hazard events.
